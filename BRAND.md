# BREAKLINE

## Brand

**BUILD. SQUAD. EXTRACT.**

Breakline is a real community for extraction-shooter players. It is not a fake social network, a bot farm, or a vanity-metric project.

### Three pillars

- 🔫 **BUILD** — loadouts, builds, workbench, economy, meta.
- 👥 **SQUAD** — LFG, friends, groups, events, finding people to play with.
- 🏃 **EXTRACT** — clips, guides, intel, challenges, leaderboards, raid stories.

## Authenticity rules

- No fake member counts.
- No fake likes or views.
- No bot accounts pretending to be players.
- No manufactured comments or activity.
- Show real numbers, even when they are small.
- Never imply affiliation with a game developer unless an official partnership exists.

## First 100 campaign

Goal: reach the first **100 real operators**.

The first 100 members are the founding community. Profiles created while the community is below 100 members receive `Founding Operator` status permanently.

### Content loop

1. Publish useful extraction-shooter content.
2. Show the real current member/build count.
3. Invite people to join Breakline.
4. Turn member activity into community content.
5. Feature real builds, squads, clips and events.
6. Repeat.

### Launch content ideas

- Day 1: “We are building an extraction shooter community from zero.”
- Day 3: “Our first operators joined.”
- Day 7: “First Breakline Budget Raid.”
- Weekly: real community count + best build + best clip + next event.

## Voice

Direct, competitive, welcoming, slightly gritty. Never corporate. Never fake hype.

Good:
> We have 27 operators. We're building from here.

Bad:
> Join the world's #1 extraction community with 50,000+ players.

## Tagline
