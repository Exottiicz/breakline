# BREAKLINE

## BUILD. SQUAD. EXTRACT.

Breakline is a community for extraction-shooter players. We are building it in public, starting small, and growing with real operators.

### The three pillars

- 🔫 **BUILD** — loadouts, builds, workbench tools, economy and meta.
- 👥 **SQUAD** — LFG, friends, groups, events and finding people to play with.
- 🏃 **EXTRACT** — clips, guides, intel, challenges, leaderboards and the stories that happen in raids.

## Authenticity rules

1. Never invent member counts.
2. Never seed fake likes, comments, LFG posts or activity.
3. Never use bots to make the community look active.
4. Show real statistics from the database.
5. Mark unreleased features as **COMING SOON**.
6. Give early members credit for actually helping build Breakline.
7. Prefer a small active community over a large inactive number.

## The First 100

The first 100 real members are the founding community.

Public campaign language:

> **WE'RE BUILDING THIS FROM ZERO.**
>
> No bots. No fake numbers. Just real operators.
>
> **100 real extraction players.**

Founding status is granted only to verified early members. It should never be used to manufacture a false sense of popularity.

## Content loop

**Player creates something → Breakline showcases it → community interacts → Breakline creates content → new player discovers Breakline → new player joins → new player creates something.**
